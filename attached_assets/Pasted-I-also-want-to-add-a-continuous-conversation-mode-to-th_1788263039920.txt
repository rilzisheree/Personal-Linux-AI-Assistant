I also want to add a **continuous conversation mode** to the existing wake-word system.

The idea is:

**I should only need to say the wake word once to start a conversation.**

For example:

```text
Me:
"Hey Jarvis, what's the weather?"

↓ Wake word detected

AI:
"It's currently 31 degrees, Sir."

↓ AI finishes speaking

Me:
"What about tomorrow?"

↓ NO wake word needed

AI:
"Tomorrow should be..."

↓ AI finishes speaking

Me:
"And what about Friday?"

↓ NO wake word needed

AI:
"Friday looks..."
```

### Conversation Mode Behavior

Once the wake word is detected, enter a **conversation session**.

While the conversation session is active:

* Do NOT require the wake word for every message.
* Automatically listen for my next response after the AI finishes speaking.
* Detect when I finish speaking using the existing VAD/end-of-speech system.
* Send my speech to STT → Ollama → TTS as normal.
* After the AI finishes speaking, automatically listen for another response.
* Continue this loop until the conversation session ends.

The flow should be:

```text
IDLE / WAKE WORD LISTENING
        ↓
Wake word detected
        ↓
CONVERSATION ACTIVE
        ↓
Listen
        ↓
User speaks
        ↓
End of speech detected
        ↓
STT
        ↓
Ollama
        ↓
TTS
        ↓
AI finishes speaking
        ↓
Listen again
        ↓
User speaks again
        ↓
...
```

### Ending the Conversation

The conversation should NOT stay active forever.

End the conversation when:

* I've been silent for a configurable amount of time after the AI finishes speaking.
* I explicitly say something like "goodbye", "stop listening", "go to sleep", etc.
* I manually exit/cancel the conversation.
* A critical microphone/audio error occurs.

When the session ends:

```text
CONVERSATION ACTIVE
        ↓
Timeout / goodbye / manual stop
        ↓
IDLE
        ↓
Wake-word detection resumes
```

### IMPORTANT — Don't confuse this with continuous listening

I do **not** want the AI constantly sending everything from my microphone to STT/Ollama.

The wake word should be what initially activates the conversation.

After activation, the assistant temporarily enters a conversational listening state where it expects my next response.

When the conversation ends, it goes back to **wake-word-only mode**.

### Prevent the AI from hearing itself

This is extremely important.

When TTS is playing:

**Do NOT treat the AI's own voice as user speech.**

The microphone/listening state should be disabled or appropriately filtered while the AI is speaking.

Only begin listening for my next response after the AI has finished speaking.

Make sure there is also a small configurable transition delay if necessary to prevent the microphone from immediately detecting the tail end of the AI's TTS audio.

### Existing functionality

Use the existing:

* Wake-word detection
* VAD/end-of-speech detection
* STT
* Ollama
* TTS
* Orb states

Do not redesign the UI.

Add this as a proper conversation/session state to the existing voice system.

The important concept is:

**Wake word starts the conversation. It should NOT be required again for every turn.**
